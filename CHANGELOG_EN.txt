v0.33.0 — Full Skill & Equipment Progression Rebalance

- All active abilities across all 14 classes now scale much more strongly with their own Skill Level 1-400.
- Global Skill Level power curve: 1=1.00x, 50≈1.54x, 100≈1.96x, 200≈2.70x, 300≈3.37x, 400=4.00x.
- Skill cooldowns now scale up to 50% reduction at Skill Level 400, making utility/evade/control progression meaningful too.
- Passive skills of the same class receive 25% Skill XP when active skills are used and can progress from 1-400.
- Mec and Engineer passive effects now scale with their own skill levels.
- V-MAX duration now grows with V-MAX Skill Level in addition to Will and permanent upgrades.
- Mec control effects, Intercept System and Self-Repair now scale with their own Skill Level.
- All equipment uses a new nonlinear 1-400 curve for defense, stats, properties and rune values.
- Gear at thresholds 50/100/200/300/400 is substantially stronger than starter gear, with much larger endgame gains.
- Classes, Proficiency gates, sets, Forge, runes, sockets, Tech Set Mk-II/Mk-III and existing character saves remain compatible.

v0.32.0 — Party Progress, Recap & Tech Set Milestone
- Party Quest Progress 2.0: active kill quests and boss kill credit are shared with party members standing in the same room. Gathering and crafting remain personal actions only.

- Salvage 4.0: salvaging the last copy of an equipment item returns runes, while socketed gems have a Smithing-scaled 25-85% recovery chance.
